About SmartMed IoT Platform

Verified Pin Map & System Architecture

  • Servos 1–3: GPIO 32 (Morning), GPIO 26 (Afternoon), GPIO 27 (Night)
  • I2C Shared Bus: SDA GPIO 21, SCL GPIO 22 (TMP117 0x48, MAX30102 0x57, DS3231 RTC 0x68, LCD 0x27)
  • Configurable GPIOs: Buzzer (default GPIO 4), Ultrasonic HC-SR04 (TRIG 5 / ECHO 17), Acknowledge Button (GPIO 16)

Safety & Biomedical Ethics

Ultrasonic sensing validates physical object presence (≤ 5 cm default) without claiming exact tablet counts or swallowing confirmation. Optical SpO₂ & pulse values are engineering estimates.
